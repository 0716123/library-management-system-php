Inter Library Loan - ILL

The Library facilitates borrowing documents that are unavailable in its collection through Interlibrary Loan from other libraries. This service operates on an Institution-to-Institution basis. Borrowed materials must be handled with the utmost care and returned promptly to the library.

This service is exclusively for academic and research purposes and is available to faculty, research scholars, students, and staff who are library members. Members are required to submit a formal request to the library, providing complete bibliographic details of the desired documents. Before making an Interlibrary Loan request, please ensure that the item is not already available in our library's collection by checking Library OPAC.

For more information, please contact the library via email at libraryservices@atmiyauni.ac.in
