Document Delivery Service DDS

The Library facilitates obtaining copies of papers from journals, conference proceedings, and other sources that are not part of its collection from various library networks and memberships. There is no additional cost for this service provided to library members. It is offered exclusively for academic and research purposes to faculty, research scholars, students, and staff who are library members.

Members are encouraged to submit a formal request to the library, including proper bibliographic details of the required documents. Please send your requests via email to: libraryservices@atmiyauni.ac.in
